A brighter Ferry that shows, up front, where it works.

Design only. Nothing in the app has changed yet. Tell me what to change, and I'll build it after you approve.

On a phone, the desktop screens are shrunk to fit. Pinch to zoom, or open this file on a computer.

What's new

  • English only. Spanish and Arabic are gone.
  • Real site logos up front: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and the rest, so people see where Ferry works.
  • One plain answer per site: download directly, official download only, or protected.
  • Live status (new): a green or orange dot from the last hour of real downloads.
  • A bolder look: sunny yellow, wide heavy type, chunky controls, downloads as tickets.

Please decide

  • Yellow: keep the big yellow top, or use yellow only on buttons?
  • Live status needs a small server addition (success counts per site, no personal data). Build it?
  • YouTube stays "official download only", as your spec's rules require. Facebook, SoundCloud and Bandcamp are the same.

  • Logos are grouped by what Ferry can do with them, with a live dot on each.
  • Try it: type or paste any link in the box, tap an example, or tap a logo.

Phone

  • Same design, made for one hand: a big Paste button, tabs at the bottom, choices in a bottom sheet.
  • Phones open Ferry from the Share menu, so the link is usually already in the box.

Design system

  • One loud colour, one typeface family, four status colours. Text on yellow is always dark ink (contrast above 12:1).
  • Every status pairs a colour with an icon and words, so it never relies on colour alone.
