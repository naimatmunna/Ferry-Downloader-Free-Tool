How Ferry decides
  • Free to save

    Public-domain and openly licensed media, or files the site offers for download. Pick a format and save.

  • Your own or with permission

    The site limits downloading in its terms. Save only what you made or have permission to save; you confirm this each time.

  • Why some sites aren’t listed

    Ferry can’t download from them: they’re protected (DRM, paywalls, sign-in), they only allow their own download tools, or they block downloads from Ferry. Paste a link from one and Ferry tells you why.
