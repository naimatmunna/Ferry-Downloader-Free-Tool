Say what happened, what works, and what to do next.

Today a failed link shows one red sentence, and for X and Facebook picture posts it wrongly says “the site may have changed”.

What changes

  • The card names the site and gives a plain title instead of a red warning.
  • For a post type Ferry can’t save yet, it says Coming soon and lists what does work on that site.
  • One main button: Paste another link, or Try again when waiting helps.

Kept honest

  • Coming soon only where Ferry could add it without signing in: pictures on X, Facebook, TikTok, and websites it can’t read yet.
  • Sites that need a sign-in (Reddit, Vimeo) or use DRM (Netflix) say so. Ferry never signs in, so “coming soon” would be a false promise.
