  • The FAQ sits under “How it works” on the Download page. The footer’s “FAQ” link jumps to it.
  • Answers use this server’s real numbers: sites, size and length limits, how long files stay.
  • The footer is on every page. On phones it sits above the tab bar.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about saving, quality and privacy.

Is Ferry free?

Yes. There’s no account and nothing to install. Paste a link, pick a format and save.

Which sites can I download from?

155 sites, including TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X and SoundCloud. See them all on the Sites page, with live status. Ferry also tries any other page with a plain video or audio file.

Can I save just the audio, like an MP3?

Yes. Choose Audio only, then MP3, M4A, FLAC or another format.

What quality will I get?

Up to the best the source has, such as 1080p or 4K. Ferry only offers qualities that really exist and never upscales.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. On iPhone and iPad, Ferry opens the file in Safari so you can save it. On Android, add Ferry to your home screen and it shows up when you tap Share in other apps.

Is there a size or length limit?

Yes. This server takes files up to 250 MB and media up to 30 minutes long.

Do you keep my files?

No. Each file leaves the server 15 minutes after it’s ready, or sooner if you turn on “Delete files from the server after saving” in Settings. Your history stays on this device. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Why can’t I download from Netflix or Spotify?

They protect their media with DRM or paywalls, and Ferry never breaks that protection. Paste a link anyway: Ferry tells you if the site has its own download option.

Is it legal to download videos?

It depends on the video. Saving your own videos, openly licensed media or anything the owner allows is generally fine. Many sites’ terms forbid saving other people’s videos, and Ferry marks those sites “With permission”. Only save what you’re allowed to.

How can I support Ferry?

Give it a star on GitHub. It’s free and helps more people find Ferry. Star on GitHub
