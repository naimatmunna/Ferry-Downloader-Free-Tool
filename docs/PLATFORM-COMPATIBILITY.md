## The five categories

| # | Category | Default behaviour | Platforms |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Officially supported downloads | Downloads allowed | 20 |
| 2 | Authorized user-owned content exports | Official download/export only — Ferry links to it | 1 |
| 3 | Public media with permitted downloading | Downloads allowed | 12 |
| 4 | Technically supported, permission-restricted | Own/licensed content only (user confirms) | 146 |
| 5 | Unsupported or protected | Not supported (protected) | 70 |

Being technically extractable never makes a platform "authorized": category 4 platforms require the
user to confirm they own the content or have the rights holder's permission, and their official
routes are always shown. By the operator's decision of 2026-11-24, YouTube, YouTube Music, Facebook,
SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Snapchat, LinkedIn and VK, previously official-export-only, are category 4 too.
Ferry still never solves YouTube's JavaScript challenges (courts in the US and Germany have treated
its signature cipher as a technical protection measure), so some YouTube formats may be missing —
see `RESEARCH.md`.

## Reliability scale

* **verified** — working extractor/API, no recurring breakage known on 2026-11-25
* **experimental** — works, but breaks periodically or often needs sign-in
* **unstable** — frequently broken upstream
* **unsupported** — no working engine path (DRM, defunct, no extractor, or engine not installed)

Live per-platform success rates (last 24 h, aggregate counts only) are served by `GET /api/v1/status`
and shown on the in-app Sites page.
